Beyond Freelancing Platforms: How Your Website Can Help You Earn More as a Content Writer

Opps wait readers just pause for a second and think about what happens when a client gets your resume in their mail inbox.They might probably Google your name.What do they find in general a LinkedIn profile maybe which you haven’t touched in a year,a couple of instagram posts and offcourse a byline or two which has already buried on someone else’s site.

Now just take a moment to visualize a different scenario, a simple website with your best samples,a short note on who you write for,and a button that says “HIRE ME”.Which writer would you pick?A website is the one place online that's completely and fully yours.Social media apps change their rules all the time and due to increasing cyber crimes rates in today’s fast paced digital world social media accounts can easily get hacked and banned. But your own domain stays put and everything you build on it keeps adding up over the years.

Do you know the best part is that a website does a lot of the selling for you.Every writer says they are absolutely great with words and storytelling but at the end a wise client wants a proof and a portfolio page that lets them read it in seconds and thoroughly in order to analyze whether they are a fit to perform or not. Therefore the writers can sort out their samples by topic like health,travel,tech and even add a line about what the piece achieved like more traffic or more shares.

If you start a blog on your site, it can show up on Google, which means clients may find you without you pitching anyone.That’s also a quiet way of showing you understand SEO, which is exactly what many clients are paying for. Add a few testimonials, a clear services page, and an About section that sounds like you, and a stranger can become a client while you’re asleep or busy with another deadline.

The best part is what a website lets you do down the road. It becomes your home base, where you can try out your own voice, start a newsletter, share free guides, or one day sell a course or ebook. Writers with a proper site also tend to look more established, and that often means they can charge more. Building one also makes you decide who you write for and what makes your style different, which helps your whole career feel clearer. And it doesn’t have to be expensive or hard. WordPress, Wix, and Squarespace can get you online in an afternoon. If you’ve been putting it off, this is your sign to start. You already have the words, so now give them somewhere to live.
